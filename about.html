Company Profile

Architecting Experiences.

EDND (Events, Manpower & Experiences) is an integrated event management and on-ground marketing execution agency established to deliver high-standard operational excellence.

Who We Are

Your Dependable Partner for Corporate Event Solutions

At EDND, we believe every corporate event, exhibition pavilion, and brand activation is a direct reflection of an organization’s prestige and vision. We operate with an unwavering commitment to precision, accountability, and seamless coordination.

Under our defining ethos — LEARN • EARN • GROW — EDND bridges strategic event design with relentless on-ground execution. Whether orchestrating a closed-door corporate AGM, commanding a multi-day conference, managing a custom exhibition booth, or mobilizing field manpower for consumer activations, our focus remains on delivering structured, stress-free outcomes.

Based in Mumbai with pan-India operational reach, we collaborate directly with corporate enterprises, event agencies requiring ground execution support, exhibition organizers, marketing agencies, universities, and consumer brands.

Our Guiding Principles

Foundations of Operational Excellence

The core tenets guiding every production timeline, technical deployment, and client collaboration at EDND.

Meticulous Planning

Every detail is mapped in advance — minute-by-minute run-of-shows, stage layouts, technical redundancy, vendor checklists, and contingency schedules.

Disciplined Manpower

Our on-ground staff, hostesses, registration coordinators, and brand ambassadors are briefed, professionally attired, and trained for corporate environments.

Turnkey Production

From heavy-duty trussing and intelligent stage lighting to high-definition LED video walls, crystal-clear audio engineering, and custom stall fabrication.

Single-Point Accountability

You are assigned a dedicated Single Point of Contact (SPOC) who oversees all vendor coordination, logistics, and on-ground delivery with direct transparency.

Adaptive Solutions

Flexible execution models suited for intimate board meetings, expansive academic symposiums, national dealer conferences, or experiential public roadshows.

Zero-Compromise Safety

Rigorous safety adherence across electrical rigging, structural stability of booths, emergency egress protocols, and event crowd flow management.
